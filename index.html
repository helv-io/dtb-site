How it works

  1. We send a small computer. You plug it into the car USB. TeslaUSB is the open-source project that makes the Pi look like a USB drive. It is not a Tesla product. The car writes SavedClips and Sentry to it.
  2. Park on home wifi. Footage leaves the car so we can render.
  3. We render. Four cameras become one clip. Not you.
  4. You get a link. Watch it. Share it.

You already have

  • A Tesla with dashcam (SavedClips / Sentry)
  • You park on home wifi
  • That wifi has a password. WPA2 or mixed WPA2/WPA3 works. A WPA3-only mesh often will not let the Pi join.
  • That wifi has internet
  • If we ship a Zero W or Zero 2 W, your home wifi needs a 2.4 GHz network

In the box

  • The computer, ready to plug in
  • 64 GB or larger card, already in it
  • A data cable. Charge-only cables fail.
  • A case
  • A USB splitter if you need the car USB port free

Your clips

Footage leaves the car so we can render. You get a link. They are your clips.
