dTb
dashcam T board

What you get

Park at home. Wake up to a finished clip.

A small computer in the car is the dashcam USB. When you are on home wifi, the footage leaves the car. We render it. You watch and share from the web.

You do not copy sticks. You do not run Docker. You do not render mosaics.

How it works

  1. In the car. A Raspberry Pi (or similar) runs TeslaUSB. The car thinks it is a USB drive. SavedClips and Sentry write to it.
  2. On home wifi. You park in range. The Pi archives the footage off the car. That wifi needs internet so the archive can reach us.
  3. We render. Four cameras become one watchable clip. That work happens on our side, not on your PC.
  4. You watch. Finished video is served from S3 (or S3-compatible storage). Watch it. Share a link.

You need this

  • A Tesla with dashcam (SavedClips / Sentry)
  • You park in range of home wifi
  • That wifi is WPA2 PSK
  • That wifi has internet (the archive has to reach us)
  • Pi Zero W and Zero 2 W are 2.4 GHz only. If that is the board, your home wifi must have a 2.4 GHz network
  • An SBC with USB OTG (Pi Zero W, Zero 2 W, Pi 4, or Pi 5 with the right cable)
  • 64 GB or larger microSD
  • A data-capable USB cable. Charge-only cables fail
  • A case
  • Optional: a USB splitter so you keep a car USB port

Hardware list is from the TeslaUSB docs, not a guess. teslausb · hardware wiki

You do not need this

  • A GPU or NVENC box at home
  • Your own rack
  • Docker
  • A DIY mosaic pipeline
